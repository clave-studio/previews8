The practice

One office, and a membership.

Courtney Garner is the founder and lead provider at WellSpot Functional Medicine in Owasso, Oklahoma. She is an APRN, CNP, and ABAAHP clinician.

Complete Care and Vital Care memberships are offered, billed monthly or prepaid annually. The initial consultation is a separate fee from the membership. The physical office is in Owasso; Florida follow-up is telemedicine after an in-person first visit.

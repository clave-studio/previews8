The practice

Twenty years of functional medicine, in a cottage on Arlington.

Dr. Susan Bradt is a family physician, an Institute for Functional Medicine Certified Practitioner, and board-certified in integrative and holistic medicine. She has practiced integrative and functional medicine in Asheville for two decades, with deep experience in bioidentical hormones, Hashimoto’s, autoimmunity, gastrointestinal disorders, mold and metals, and brain health.

Amanda Johnson, ANP-C, provides the same unhurried, root-cause care as a functional medicine nurse practitioner. Together they listen to the full timeline, run the tests that actually explain the pattern, and build a plan with you — nutrition, lifestyle, nutraceuticals, and medication when it’s the right tool.

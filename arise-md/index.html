The practice

A surgeon who learned, as a patient.

Dr. Miller is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon and an integrative medicine physician. After altitude sickness on Mt. Baker ended a night in a Vancouver ER, he rebuilt his own health and then the practice: ARISE MD, in Mequon, for people who are successful and still exhausted.

The model is TOP Health — treat what is present with the best of Asia and the Americas, optimize, then prevent what comes next. A first visit includes a head-to-toe exam, skin and oral cancer screening, a mini stress test, and a high-sensitivity metabolic lab panel, with at least three things you can do the next day.

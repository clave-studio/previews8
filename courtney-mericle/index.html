The practice

Coaching for women who want their edge back.

Courtney Mericle is a certified life coach, breathwork facilitator, and nutrition expert based in Denver. The SHIFT Project is her 1:1 coaching practice for women who want to reclaim energy, confidence, and vitality — not another generic plan.

SHIFT is a six-month program. SHIFT VIP is a more intensive six-month container. Courtney also reads every message personally and aims to respond within 48 hours.
